# DR-0008 · Charter mother — release `v0.1.3`: corrections under `49GAION/DR-0052`

- **Date:** 2026-10-29
- **Decided by:** the governing seat (GOV) of 49GAION.
- **Source:**
  - The governing seat's word of 29 October 2026 (translated from Persian): "Check it too: see what is useful and what needs a decision, then build it all at once; combine both and go through one correction round."
  - `49GAION/DR-0052`: two reviews of `v2.1.0` checked against the complete definition and corrected in one round; each mother touched takes a patch version by its own record, citing it.
  - `49GAION/DR-0053`, item 11: the governing seat's ruling of 29 October 2026 (translated from Persian): "It can give its Chief of Staff full access on its own behalf, even to sign. But that does not mean that AI decides." A position grants only to its own Chief of Staff, and the decision stays the position's.
  - The record is prepared by the AI hub; the governing seat's merge is the decision.

## Decision

1. **Corrected.** The mother blueprint takes `v0.1.3`: top management sits in the Top Management Group, the first AIOC under the core, not in the Charter, which names it where it signs, owns a test or receives a raise; its card leaves the Charter (the complete definition, §19); the Chief of Staff signs nothing in its own name, and for a position only within that position's grant (`49GAION/DR-0053`, item 11). Each text now says what the complete definition says; no rule of this part is added or dropped. The wording replaced is listed below, verbatim.
2. **Cards.** Every card takes `v0.1.3`, generated by `tools/make_cards.py` from the blueprint, against the schemas of the Operating Protocol at the pin.
3. **The Protocol pin.** `PROTOCOL-PIN` and every `uses:` line of the checks move to the Operating Protocol `v2.2.0` (`afc91b6`), and the checks gain `links`: every relative link resolves.
4. **The Library pin.** `49GAION-protocol` `library/README.md` moves its pin of this blueprint to `v0.1.3` by its own pull request, once this one is merged.
