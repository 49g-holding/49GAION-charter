## Decision

1. **What was found.** The core holds the Records Pod and the Work Pod of this blueprint, while the complete definition says the `[ORG]AION` plans the operation and never runs it (the definition, see 12). A reader could take holding the shared queue as running the operation.
2. **The reading.** The Records and Work Pods are shared services that run as code, like the gateway. Holding them is not running the operation: every record and every Work Item stays with the unit that owns it, and the Charter's AI Cells keep their limits (they watch, route and raise). Where an organisation places these services later is decided by AI at the time, with the governance seat's approval.
3. **The version.** Unreleased. The blueprint stays at `v0.1.1`, its cards do not change, and the Library pin does not move.
