# DR-0003 · Charter mother: the readings of 49GAION/DR-0041

- **Date:** 2026-10-25
- **Decided by:** the governing seat (GOV) of 49GAION
- **Source:** `49GAION/DR-0041`, the complete definition of 49GAION (`49GAION-FRAMEWORK.md`), approved by the governing seat on 25 October 2026, and its direction of the same day (translated): "Go to all the repositories; remove nothing; where something is contrary to it, bring it into line, and put this definition into them."
- **Relation:** builds on DR-0002; it supersedes nothing and removes nothing.

## Decision

1. **The complete definition governs.** The complete definition of 49GAION, approved by its governing seat on 25 October 2026, is `49GAION-FRAMEWORK.md` in `github.com/49g-holding/49GAION` (`49GAION/DR-0041`). Where this repository differs from it, the definition governs, and this repository is read with it until its next version brings it into line. Nothing here is removed.
2. **A pattern of the Library.** This text is a pattern of the 49GAION Library, the greatest asset of every `[ORG]AION`. An organisation takes it, personalises it or builds something better, and what it takes is held in its own Library, at its governance level, as its own rules (the definition, see 9 and 14). 49GAION writes principles and the few safeguards that always hold (the definition, see 20). Where this text sets a detail or a way of working, that detail is the pattern's default: the organisation's `[ORG]AION` decides it at the time and may change it, within the safeguards and with the approval the organisation's own rules require.
3. **The core.** The Charter is the Library part from which an organisation's core is made. The core is the root (Standard §1.7, item 13): the first AIOC, at the governance level. It holds the human position of the governance seat, which its AI serves and whose powers it never holds; the organisation's own Standard, Blueprint and Library, its IP; the governance panel; and the one tool gateway and the stop, which run as code outside every AI (the definition, see 13). What this blueprint sets for the Gateway, Records and Work Pods stands.
4. **Top Management.** The Top Management position sits in the Top Management Group, the first AIOC under the core. The Cell `[ORG]CELL-TMS` and its card are kept. Where this blueprint gives top management an act in the Charter (its signature with the governing seat on gateway policy, the tests it owns, the raises it receives), Top Management takes that act from the Top Management Group, through the gateway, under live sign-in.
5. **The hub of the operation.** Every rule here that names `[ORG]AIOC-OCO`, the Operating Core Office, or the human hub is read with the definition (see 19): the Top Management Group, the first AIOC under the core, is the hub of the operation, both the human hub and the AI hub, and builds everything in the operation, or plans it, or puts its tools in place. 49GAION requires no separate AI hub unit; where an organisation has one, `[ORG]AIOC-OCO` does this work under Top Management. Where the organisation has none, "never by `[ORG]AIOC-OCO` alone" reads "never by any AI alone".
6. **The work of the core's AI.** At the governance level the core's AI does the work of the `[ORG]AION`: the first proposal, from 49GAION's patterns and the governance seat's brief; the review of every proposal sent to it, rejected with a reason or applied with the governance seat's approval or within the dial that seat has signed; and following the organisation and reading its record for that seat (the definition, see 13 and 14). It proposes and never decides for a seat. How this work is arranged beside the Pods of this blueprint is decided by AI at the time. The Charter's AI Cells keep their limits: they watch, route and raise, and no AI holds the keys.
7. **Changes and installations.** A change to the core itself is prepared by the core's AI and signed by the governing seat, as this blueprint already sets. An installation in the operation is prepared by the Top Management Group, or by a separate `[ORG]AIOC-OCO` under Top Management where one exists, and signed as this blueprint sets.
8. **Governance and Delegation.** The acts of the second capability (the mandate to Top Management and its envelope, the gate, the governance veto and the dial) are the governance seat's own acts, each recorded; no AI takes any of them (the definition, see 15).
9. **Panel and back office.** The panel is the unit's interface: the organisation's own, written in code, made from the 49GAION patterns, from which every task of the unit can be controlled. The back office is the AI chat workspace where the unit's AI works: an authorised interface too, through the same controls, and the keeper of the panel, also after the panel is built, so no separate admin back office is written. Each covers the other: if the panel fails, the work goes on from the back office, which can rebuild it. The back office is never the route of a human port, a stop door, a signature or the accountability path (the definition, see 17).
10. **Positions.** Every human seat here is a position, never a person. The governance seat appoints Top Management directly and keeps the final right over who holds any position; Top Management fills the positions below it in its own mechanism. One person may hold several positions where the law allows; the positions stay distinct (the definition, see 6).
11. **The delivery test.** Blueprint §2.1 names "delivery test D-2" in `floor_bindings`. The delivery test is withdrawn (`49GAION/DR-0032`); the binding of each Human Floor item to a seat stays a rule of its own (Standard §1.5).
12. **Powered by 49GAION.** An organisation that takes this part, or any other item of 49GAION, carries "Powered by 49GAION" on all its products (the definition, see 11).
13. **The version.** Unreleased. The blueprint stays at `v0.1.1`, its cards do not change, and the Library pin does not move. The readings are folded into the text at its next version, which reaches the Library only by a pull request that moves the pin, on the governing seat's word.
