# DR-0005 · Charter mother: the readings of 49GAION/DR-0042

- **Date:** 2026-10-25
- **Decided by:** the governing seat (GOV) of 49GAION
- **Source:** `49GAION/DR-0042`, the clarifications of the complete definition of 49GAION, approved by the governing seat on 25 October 2026.
- **Relation:** builds on DR-0003 and DR-0004; it supersedes nothing and removes nothing.

## Decision

1. **The gateway and the stop beside the core.** The Gateway Pod and the stop are installed with the core but run as code beside it, outside the reach of every AI, the core's own included; the Charter's AI Cells keep their limits. The core's own stop is held directly by the governance seat, as case 51 of TEST-CH already sets for the restart.
2. **Changes to the gateway and the stop.** Any change to the code of the gateway or the stop counts as loosening: it needs a human signature (`change-gateway-policy`), and it is made by someone other than the AI it constrains.
3. **Fresh keys at the handover.** At the handover the organisation issues fresh keys for its gateway, and every key Edition Maker made, held or used is revoked.
4. **The live sign-in and a standing approval.** A fresh live sign-in is needed for each signature. A standing approval is signed once, with a fresh live sign-in, and covers only changes that can be undone and do not widen access, budget or authority; the gateway refuses a widening under it.
5. **The governance seat's own conflict of interest.** When the governance seat has a conflict of interest, the founding configuration names in advance either an independent position that decides in its place or a second, independent signature the decision also needs. A conflicted seat signing alone is refused at the gateway, and each recusal is reported to the independent internal-audit line (HF-19).
6. **The authority ceiling.** Every Work Item carries its original requester, the sender of the first Work Item of its chain; the gateway runs an act only within the authority of both the unit that does it and the original requester, however it is routed.
7. **The Chief of Staff orders and summarises, never drops.** `[ORG]CELL-COSCH` may order and summarise the raises it collates; it never drops or delays one, and the raw record is always open to the human seat it serves.
8. **"Always."** Where this blueprint says that something always holds, it holds in every edition 49GAION hands over; after the handover it is the organisation's own, within the law.
9. **The fixed test.** TEST-CH gains cases 52 to 58, every one `NOT_RUN`, for items 1 to 7. Cases 52 to 56 are must-fail cases: each passes only when the gateway refuses the act.
10. **The version.** Unreleased. The blueprint stays at `v0.1.1`, its cards do not change, and the Library pin does not move.
